How it works

Three steps, then it stays out of your way.

  1. Make a category

    Give it a name and a color. Under Opens with, add the apps this kind of work needs. Search installed apps like the Start menu, or pick from windows that are open right now.

  2. Set where apps open

    Open the app, drag it where you want it, click Capture position. No typing coordinates. Catogarizer remembers size, position and monitor.

  3. Switch

    Press Ctrl + Alt + Space, type a few letters or press a number, hit Enter. Tap the hotkey twice to jump back to where you were.

Details that matter

Built to be trusted with your windows.

Nothing gets closed

Switching only hides windows. Your unsaved work stays exactly as it was, and everything comes back when you return.

Lost a window? It's recoverable

One click in the tray brings every hidden window back. They also return when Catogarizer exits, or on next start after a crash.

Hold back distractions

List apps a category should block. They're closed as soon as they start, and you get a notification. No admin rights needed.

Always-visible apps

Pin a music player or a chat assistant so it stays on screen in every category and is never hidden by a switch.

Anything you open joins in

Open something while a category is active and it belongs to that category. You never have to file windows by hand.

Lives in the tray

Closing the main window only hides it, so the hotkey keeps working. Small footprint, no bundled browser engine.

FAQ

Good to know.

Does it need administrator rights?

No. It uses standard Windows window APIs and runs as a normal user.

Are my windows really safe?

Yes. Catogarizer never closes what it hides. If it crashes or the PC loses power, hidden windows are brought back the next time it starts, and the tray has a "Show all hidden windows" button.

Can an app be in more than one category?

Yes. Slack can live in both Deep Work and Comms, for example.

Is the app blocking a hard lock?

No, it's a soft block. It checks about three times a second, so a held-back app can flash open for a moment before it's closed.

What about Mac and Linux?

Catogarizer relies on Windows-specific window management, so it's Windows-only for now.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your PC. Catogarizer doesn't need an account and doesn't send your categories or windows anywhere.
